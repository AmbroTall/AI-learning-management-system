## Title
Freelance B2B Sales Rep — AI Training Platform (10% Commission + Path to Retainer)

## Description

**LearnPulse.online** is an AI training and certification platform. Organisations license it to upskill their workforce in AI and prompt engineering — self-paced courses, real-time AI-evaluated assessments, and verified certificates, deployed under a bulk organisation account with its own admin dashboard.

**The project**

I need a freelancer who can find organisations that need AI training and bring them to a signed license — corporates, training institutions, and government agencies. You identify prospects, make the first approach, and run the conversation through to close. I handle the demo/negotiation support and contract once you've got a decision-maker engaged.

**Payment terms**

- **10% commission on every license you close.** Deals typically run **$1,625–$7,000+** depending on seat count, so a single closed deal is worth roughly **$160–$700** to you (average first deal ≈ $375 commission).
- Paid on receipt of the client's payment, per deal.
- **After 3 months of consistent activity, strong performers move to a basic monthly retainer plus retained commission** — this is meant to become an ongoing engagement, not a one-off gig.
- Full product training, sales materials (one-pager + call scripts), and live demo access provided before you make your first approach.
- Fully remote, your own hours — this is about outcomes, not hours logged.

**Who I'm looking for**

- You've sold B2B before — software, training, or services, anything with a decision-maker and a procurement process.
- Comfortable with cold outreach; you don't need to be chased for activity.
- You can talk about AI at a business level — you don't need to be technical, you need to understand what an L&D manager or HR director is actually worried about (adoption, measurable outcomes, cost vs. Coursera/Udemy Business).
- Existing relationships in corporate L&D, HR, or public-sector training are a strong advantage.

**To apply, send a short message covering:**
1. What you've sold before.
2. Who you'd approach first for this (a real type of organisation, not a generic answer).
3. How you'd open that first conversation.

Applications missing any of those three won't be reviewed.

### Note on posting this to PeoplePerHour
PPH is built around discrete freelance projects/hourlies rather than open-ended commission roles, and its terms restrict using it as a straight recruitment/job board. Two ways to make this fit cleanly:
- Post it as a **fixed-price project** ("Find and close 1 AI-training license deal") with the fee set per deal at your commission amount, and re-post/renew for the next deal — keeps you compliant and still lets you build a repeat relationship with someone who does well.
- Or post as a **short discovery task** ("Source and qualify 10 target organisations for an AI training platform, $X flat fee") to find people worth trialling on the full commission arrangement off-platform.
I'd recommend the first option since it keeps the commission structure intact and PPH-compliant.

1. What You're Selling

LearnPulse is a gamified AI skills platform where students learn how to use AI tools effectively through hands-on challenges. Every submission is evaluated in real-time by Claude (Anthropic's AI), giving students instant, personalised feedback — not a multiple-choice quiz, but actual AI evaluation of their work.

The core value proposition: Students don't just read about AI — they practise with it, get scored on it, earn points, and unlock progressively harder challenges. It's the difference between watching a cooking show and actually cooking.

2. How You Make Money

The platform supports two revenue streams, both already built and ready to activate.

💳 B2C — Individual Subscriptions

Students pay monthly or yearly via the Network Global payment gateway. Plans are managed in your admin panel. You control pricing, duration, and features listed per plan.

🏢 B2B — Organisation Licences

You sell bulk access to schools, colleges, bootcamps, and corporate training teams. You create the organisation in your admin panel, set a max student cap, and the admin manages their own members.

3. Who to Sell To

B2C — Individual Buyers

  • Career changers — people moving into tech, data, or AI roles who need practical skills fast
  • IT students — university and college students supplementing their degree with industry-relevant AI skills
  • Working professionals — office workers, marketers, project managers who want to use AI better at work
  • IT Certify students — already warm leads; they're enrolled with you and need AI skills to get hired
  • Freelancers — developers, designers, writers who want to charge more by offering AI-enhanced services

B2B — Organisation Buyers

  • Coding bootcamps — add AI skills as a module to their existing curriculum
  • IT training companies — package LearnPulse as an add-on to CompTIA / Microsoft cert courses
  • Colleges and universities — supplement computing or business degrees with practical AI lab work
  • Corporate L&D teams — upskill employees in AI tools (HR, marketing, ops, finance)
  • Government workforce programmes — digital skills initiatives often have budget for platforms like this

5. Marketing Channels (Prioritised)

🔥 Highest ROI — Start Here

  • IT Certify student base: Email your existing students. They already trust you. Offer a founding-member discount (e.g. 40% off yearly plan for the first 3 months).
  • LinkedIn content: Post one "AI tip of the day" per weekday. Link to a free challenge on LearnPulse. Build authority then sell.
  • YouTube / TikTok shorts: 60-second videos — "Can you prompt AI better than a professional?" — show the challenge, show the score. Curiosity-driven.

📈 Medium Term — Scale What Works

  • SEO content: Blog posts targeting "how to use ChatGPT for work", "AI prompt engineering course", "best AI skills course 2025". Each post ends with a free challenge CTA.
  • Partnerships: Reach out to 10 coding bootcamps and offer a white-label or reseller deal. They add LearnPulse to their curriculum; you split revenue or offer a flat licence fee.
  • Reddit & Discord communities: r/learnprogramming, r/artificial, AI Discord servers. Participate genuinely, then mention the free module.
  • Google Ads: Target "AI course", "prompt engineering training", "learn AI skills" with a free-trial offer. Set a small daily budget (£10–20/day) and measure cost-per-registration.

🏢 B2B Sales Process

  1. Identify decision-makers at 20 target schools/bootcamps (LinkedIn, their websites)
  2. Send a cold email: "We built an AI skills platform your students will love — want 5 free seats to trial it this week?"
  3. Book a 20-minute demo call. Screen-share the platform, run through a challenge live.
  4. Send a one-page proposal with your pricing table (see Section 2 above)
  5. Close via email/invoice. Provision access in your admin panel immediately.

Key Objection Responses

"I can learn this for free on YouTube"

You can watch someone drive a car on YouTube too. LearnPulse makes you get in the car. AI evaluates your actual work — not a quiz, real feedback.

"Is it worth the money?"

One AI skill applied at work saves hours per week. At £20/month, that's pennies against the salary uplift or freelance rate increase you get from the certificate.

"I don't have time"

Challenges take 10–20 minutes. Most students do one a day. 90 days to complete the full course.

"How is this different from ChatGPT?"

ChatGPT is the tool. LearnPulse teaches you how to use it expertly — like the difference between having a piano and knowing how to play it.
